Frequently asked questions

What people most often ask before booking a Feng Shui audit, a reading or a course.

Feng Shui audits

风水勘察

Looking for our thinking on destiny itself — is it superstition, knowing fate versus accepting it? See the articles.

01How much does a Feng Shui audit cost?

Fees depend on the type of space (home, shoplot, office, factory, land for a new build), its size and the site itself — there is no one-size price.

Message us on WhatsApp with the type of space, rough size and location, and we will send a quote and arrange a time.

02What do I need to prepare?

The birth details (date and time of birth) of the owner or the person running the business, the address, and a floor plan of the house or unit.

Because we match the building to the chart, the details of the people who actually live or work there are essential.

03Do I have to renovate or knock down walls?

Not necessarily. Adjustments focus on position and layout: the main door, beds, stove, desks, cashier and so on.

Whether any building work is needed depends on the site and the chart — the plan tells you what to do first and what can wait.

04Can you do a rented home or office?

Yes. Rented premises can be adjusted just as well, focusing on the positions and arrangements you are able to change.

05Do I need to buy Feng Shui ornaments or cures?

Our plans are built on the chart together with position and layout. If a particular case calls for anything else, the report explains why, and the decision is yours.

06Should it be checked again after renovating or moving?

Once a new build or renovation is finished, we make a second visit to confirm the positions were set up as planned.

If you later move, renovate substantially, or the household changes, a fresh review is worth doing.

07Which areas do you cover?

A Feng Shui audit is done on site — measuring the facing and reading the exterior and interior. Message us from anywhere in Malaysia and we will arrange a date and travel.

Readings & date selection

论命与择日
08What does a chart reading need? What if I don't know my birth time?

Your date of birth (Gregorian or lunar) and time of birth.

If you are unsure of the time, give us the rough window; during the consultation we check it against things that have already happened in your life to settle the most likely hour.

09What can I ask in a reading?

Relationships and marriage, career and partnerships, money and health — covering lifetime matters, ten-year cycles and the year ahead.

Listing your two or three most pressing questions beforehand helps you make the most of the time.

10How does birth-date selection work?

Within your due-date window, and matched to the slots your hospital can actually offer, we select the most favourable time of birth.

Contact us early — the more dates available, the better the choice.

Yes. The Full Course starts from the basics and plotting a chart, and works up through the Four Transformations, life cycles and practical reading.

The Full Course and the Mastery Course are four days each, six hours a day, taught in Mandarin, one-to-one or as a group.

Classes are held in Taman Connaught, Cheras, Kuala Lumpur; other areas can be arranged.

14Who teaches the course?

Harry Ng, who has studied Si Hua (Flying Star) Zi Wei Dou Shu since 2016 and applies the same system to Zi Wei Feng Shui in practice. Every class is demonstrated on real charts students bring on the day.

15How much is the course, and when is the next intake?

Please ask on WhatsApp for fees and the next intake — we will reply based on the format you choose (one-to-one or group).

16Is there a certificate? Can I re-attend?

The Full Course awards a certificate of completion and the Mastery Course a Mastery certificate (the Full Course is required before Mastery). After completing a course you can re-attend for free, for life.
