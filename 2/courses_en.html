Why learn it yourself

为什么要自己学

· What you pay for is someone else's judgement

Every time you sit across from a destiny consultant, before you even pay, you have already surrendered a critical right: the authority to interpret your own fate. This is supposed to be an equal transaction, yet the only true variable is whether that consultant's worldview, values, and depth of insight occupy the same dimension as yours.

Consider the concept of "expansion": For a small neighborhood storefront, this might manifest as taking over the unit next door to increase shop floor area. For a multinational trader, it could mean securing a regional distributorship or expanding operations into neighboring countries. If a consultant's perspective and dimension are limited, their reading of the exact same "fortune or misfortune" will yield real-world implications that are fundamentally worlds apart.

Therefore, mastering this system yourself does not mean you must practice it professionally. Its genuine value lies in reclaiming the active power to interpret your own life. Moving forward, you may still seek external references, but you will never again be a helpless bystander blankly waiting for answers in a state of confusion—because whether a situation is favorable or not, you will already hold your own yardstick.

· What you actually get in four days

Not a pile of conclusions to memorise, but a working method you can run yourself:

  • A correct understanding of metaphysics, and of how it actually operates within the natural order.
  • The right attitude in front of your own destiny — knowing when to take and when to let go.
  • The ability to tell the difference between street-fortune-teller talk and a properly structured reading.
  • Freedom from the frame of fatalism, so you can reshape yourself and your own trajectory toward what you actually want.

While some students choose to practice professionally upon completing the course, the majority do so purely to establish an authentic foundation in destiny analysis. This underlying "correct knowledge and right perspective" will benefit you for a lifetime—once you grasp this core logic, you can independently adjust the Feng Shui energy fields around you and accurately read the timing to plan ahead and act decisively.

More importantly, when you recognize that the tides of fortune are against you, it grants you the wisdom to pause at the right moment, endure patiently, and gather your strength. Whether your goal is to practice professionally to help others or to focus on your own self-cultivation, both reasons for learning are entirely valid.

· Why we only teach on real charts

Why do we teach exclusively on the real charts students hand in on the day? Because a celebrity's life is already public knowledge, and fitting theory onto public information is really just writing the working backwards from a known answer. Classes use only the charts submitted in the room — the lives of the people present, or of people close to them. Whether the method holds up or falls apart is settled on the spot. Every reading is unrehearsed and impossible to fake, and that insistence on live proof is the heart of how we teach.
